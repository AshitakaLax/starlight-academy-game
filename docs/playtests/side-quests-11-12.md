# Side Quests 11–12: Desktop and Learner Playtest

Automated checks cover the curated scenarios, tiered generators, hints, progression gates, checkpoint recovery, replay behavior, one-time rewards, and the standalone HTML build. A real desktop and learner playtest is still required.

## Before starting

- Open the generated `starlight-academy.html` locally in desktop Chrome or Edge.
- Complete Chapter 5, then open Demeter's Enchanted Greenhouse.
- Confirm the two quests appear as optional activities only after Chapter 6 is complete.
- Confirm Chapter 7 remains available without completing either optional quest.

## Quest 11 — The Life-Cycle Time Traveler

- Try Foundation, Core, and Stretch questions.
- Check that each clue shows a later plant stage and asks which stage came immediately before it.
- Confirm choices use the five stages in the curated flowering-plant cycle.
- Ask Professor Hoot for the nudge, strategy, and demonstration; check that the guidance explains moving backward one step.
- Save and leave midway, resume, complete, and replay. Confirm progress is restored and the Life-Cycle Timeglass reward is not granted a second time.

## Quest 12 — The Greenhouse Recipe Restorer

- Try Foundation, Core, and Stretch questions. The available recipe clues should grow from one missing-input pattern to all three.
- Confirm the card shows two inputs already present and the greenhouse observation, without revealing the missing answer.
- Confirm the missing input is always one of sunlight, water, or carbon dioxide. Oxygen and mineral nutrients may appear as distractors, but must not be treated as photosynthesis inputs.
- Ask Professor Hoot for all three hint stages and check that the demonstration distinguishes inputs from the oxygen output.
- Save and leave midway, resume, complete, and replay. Confirm progress is restored and the Greenhouse Recipe Crystal reward is not granted a second time.

## General checks

- Use mouse, keyboard, and Tab/Enter navigation for answer choices.
- Confirm question wording and custom plant visual captions agree.
- Confirm optional quests do not add steps to Chapter 6's required sequence.
- Confirm Chapter 7 starts after Chapter 6 even when both side quests remain untouched.
- Check that the Star Gem and Starlight Sparkle totals update once for each new quest reward.
