# Side Quests 9–10: Chapter 5 playtest notes

## Scope

These optional quests unlock after Chapter 5, **Artemis and the Magical Habitats**, is complete. They do not add steps to the required Chapter 5 sequence and do not block Chapter 6.

## Quest 9 — The Adaptation Pair Detective

**Type:** Mastery extension  
**Skills:** habitats, adaptations  
**Activity:** use two adaptation observations from one mystery animal to identify the creature that matches both.

Review points:
- Confirm both observations belong to the same animal in the curated habitat lexicon.
- Check that the mystery heading and pawprint symbol do not reveal the answer.
- Test Foundation, Core, and Stretch animal pools and the varied structural/behavioral pairs.
- Confirm Professor Hoot's hints prompt learners to verify both observations.

**Reward:** Adaptation Detective Lens, 60 Star Gems, 4 Starlight Sparkles, claimed once.

## Quest 10 — The Ecosystem Cause Tracker

**Type:** Application  
**Skills:** habitats  
**Activity:** infer which curated animal action could cause an observed ecosystem effect in a described habitat.

Review points:
- Confirm all four curated ecosystem connections appear across tiered deterministic seeds.
- Verify each effect and habitat context are shown without displaying the correct action.
- Confirm all answer choices are distinct curated actions.
- Confirm Professor Hoot's hints guide learners from the observed effect back to a plausible cause.

**Reward:** Ecosystem Field Lens, 65 Star Gems, 4 Starlight Sparkles, claimed once.

## Progression and replay

- Both quests remain locked until Chapter 5 completion.
- Chapter 6 can start while quests 9–10 are still incomplete.
- Each quest saves its own checkpoint and completion/replay history.
- Replaying a completed quest does not grant its reward again.

Automated regression coverage checks tier determinism, animal/adaptation membership, ecosystem cause/effect matching, hint stages, progression gating, and once-only rewards. A real desktop browser and learner playtest is still required.
