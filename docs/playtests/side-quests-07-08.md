# Side Quests 7–8: Chapter 4 playtest notes

## Scope

These optional quests unlock after Chapter 4, **Geometry on the Balance Beam**, is complete. They are not inserted into the required Chapter 4 sequence and do not block Chapter 5.

## Quest 7 — The Sky Gallery Shape Riddles

**Type:** Mastery extension  
**Skills:** shape recognition, symmetry, angles  
**Activity:** identify a two-dimensional shape by combining its side count, angle clue, and number of lines of symmetry.

Review points:
- Confirm all three clues agree with the target shape across Foundation, Core, and Stretch.
- Check that the angle clue distinguishes a rectangle from a rhombus.
- Confirm the diagram uses a neutral “mystery shape” label so it does not reveal the answer to assistive technology.
- Confirm Professor Hoot's three hints encourage checking every property.

**Reward:** Sky Gallery Compass, 55 Star Gems, 3 Starlight Sparkles, claimed once.

## Quest 8 — The Balance Beam Builders

**Type:** Application  
**Skills:** perimeter, area  
**Activity:** use real-world platform stories to decide whether the task measures the outside boundary or the inside surface, then calculate the result.

Review points:
- Confirm ribbon and fencing stories use perimeter.
- Confirm tile and mosaic stories use area.
- Check rectangle dimensions, numeric answer choices, units, and the rectangle diagram.
- Confirm Professor Hoot's hints distinguish “around” from “cover.”

**Reward:** Builder's Star Toolkit, 60 Star Gems, 4 Starlight Sparkles, claimed once.

## Progression and replay

- Both quests remain locked until Chapter 4 completion.
- Chapter 5 can start while quests 7–8 are still incomplete.
- Each quest saves its own checkpoint and completion/replay history.
- Replaying a completed quest does not grant its reward again.

Automated regression coverage checks tier determinism, geometric clues, measurement calculations, use of both perimeter and area stories, hint stages, progression gating, and once-only rewards. A real desktop browser and learner playtest is still required.
