# Side Quests 5–6: Chapter 3 playtest notes

## Scope

These optional quests unlock after Chapter 3, **Spellbook of Spelling & Root Words**, is complete. They do not add activities to the required Chapter 3 sequence and do not block Chapter 4.

## Quest 5 — The Morphology Looking Glass

**Type:** Mastery extension  
**Skills:** prefixes, roots, suffixes  
**Activity:** analyze a curated target word and select the correctly ordered sequence of its word parts.

Review points:
- Confirm prefix, root, and suffix components are presented in reading order.
- Check the Foundation, Core, and Stretch word sets, including multi-part Stretch words.
- Check the reusable spelling note: the final e in use is dropped before -able.
- Confirm Professor Hoot's three hint stages are useful without directly giving away the answer.

**Reward:** Wordsmith's Prism, 45 Star Gems, 3 Starlight Sparkles, claimed once.

## Quest 6 — The Missing Word Library

**Type:** Application  
**Skills:** prefixes, roots, suffixes  
**Activity:** use a curated word's meaning and a sentence with one blank to select the word that restores the sentence.

Review points:
- Confirm the target word is removed from the displayed sentence.
- Verify that the definition clue and sentence context agree with the answer.
- Check all tier-specific choices are real entries from the curated lexicon.
- Confirm Professor Hoot's hints guide learners to use both meaning and context.

**Reward:** Storykeeper's Quill, 50 Star Gems, 3 Starlight Sparkles, claimed once.

## Progression and replay

- Both quests remain locked until Chapter 3 completion.
- Chapter 4 can start while quests 5–6 are still incomplete.
- Each quest saves its own checkpoint and completion/replay record.
- Replaying a completed quest does not grant its reward again.

Automated regression coverage checks tier determinism, lexicon membership, answer uniqueness, hint stages, progression gating, and once-only reward claims. A real desktop browser and learner playtest is still required.
