# Side quests 19–20: Chapter 10 playtest notes

## The Starlight Skill Matchmaker

**Type:** Mastery extension  
**Activity:** Read a short championship skill clue and identify the matching star from mathematics, science, and language arts.

Difficulty changes the distractors: Foundation choices come from other subjects, Core includes one same-subject distractor, and Stretch compares four stars from the same subject. The question bank uses all twelve unique stars from the existing Grand Starlight sampler.

**Reward:** Starlight Skill Lens — 95 Star Gems and 6 Starlight Sparkles, claimable once.

## The Grand Constellation Planner

**Type:** Practical application  
**Activity:** Plan a balanced board with four mathematics, four science, and four language-arts stars.

Foundation asks how many stars are missing from one subject. Core asks for the total missing spaces across all three subjects. Stretch starts with twelve selected stars and asks how many selections must be replaced when the subject counts are over- or under-filled.

**Reward:** Grand Constellation Keepsake — 100 Star Gems and 7 Starlight Sparkles, claimable once.

## Regression coverage

Automated tests cover all twelve championship events, tier-specific distractor balance, generated subject counts, deterministic seeded output, correct answers, hints, checkpoint/resume, replay, and one-time rewards. The two quests unlock only after Chapter 10 is complete; the main twelve-event championship, ribbon routine, academy showcase, and crown award remain unchanged.
